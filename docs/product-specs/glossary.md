# Glossary

- **Current truth** — facts about what exists and works now; summarized in `docs/STATE.md` and backed by executable evidence.
- **Binding decision** — a still-active choice that constrains future work; stored in `docs/DECISIONS.md`.
- **Design Doc** — significant proposal used when competing structural approaches have meaningful consequences.
- **Exec Plan** — durable execution plan for migrations, cutovers, or multi-session work.
- **Archive** — read-only historical material that is never current authority.
- **Ducker** — default final human approval authority for this template and derived projects unless explicitly superseded by a project's own binding decision.
